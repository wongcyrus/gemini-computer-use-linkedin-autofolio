Esther is the heart of our student developer community at HKIIT. She holds the unique distinction of serving concurrently in three major ambassador and community leadership roles:

Beyond campus, Esther genuinely cares about using technology to serve the wider community. She volunteered with **CLP Power Hong Kong (中華電力)** to teach elderly citizens practical, everyday uses of generative AI, and she is always the first to volunteer as a student ambassador at **HKIIT Info Days** and institutional media features (*「與其怕AI，不如讀AI」*), sharing her learning journey to inspire secondary school students.

### 2. Public Speaking, Tech Evangelism & Collaborative Project Showcases
Rather than working in isolation, Esther thrives in collaborative project teams where she serves as the communicator, coordinator, and live presenter who translates technical concepts into engaging demonstrations for diverse audiences:
* **VTC × Microsoft Hong Kong MoU Signing Ceremony Presenter (Aug 2026):** Because of her poise, reliability, and clear communication skills, we selected Esther as the student presenter to represent HKIIT in front of Microsoft executives and VTC leadership, where she demonstrated our team’s *ProvePresent* and *AI Virtual Tutor* projects.
* **PyCon Hong Kong 2026 Co-Speaker (Nov 2026):** I invited Esther to co-present with Arthur Chin and me on *「從港域時空到 Kubernetes: 將香港遊戲世界化身雲原生學習平台」 (From Hong Kong Zero to Kubernetes)*. While the core backend engineering was a collaborative team and mentor effort, Esther played a vital role in testing the student learning experience, preparing the demonstration workflow, and sharing the stage to communicate how gamified learning helps beginners understand Kubernetes.
* **Active Presenter Across Hong Kong’s Tech Community:** Esther has fearlessly taken the stage at numerous community events to share our student team demos—including presenting *ProvePresent* (an Azure AI anti-proxy attendance concept) at the **Hong Kong Open Source Conference (HKOSCon 2026)**, showcasing an AI Agent robotics demo in the Developer Zone at **AWS Summit Hong Kong 2026**, demonstrating *DOOM on Kubernetes* at the **AWS User Group Hong Kong Meetup**, and assisting at **Google Cloud Leaders Connect** and **GDGHK Build with AI** at the Google Hong Kong office.
